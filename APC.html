About APC Explorer

This page displays annual APC list prices from 14 scholarly publishers between 2019 and 2025. The dataset contains 12,540 journals and 69,856 journal-year records. These are listed prices, not amounts paid; coverage depends on the publisher price lists and archived pages that could be retrieved.

Dataset: Matthias, L., Chavarro, D., Schares, E., Alperin, J. P., Rose, M., Frost, M., et al. (2026). A dataset of article processing charges from 14 scholarly publishers, 2019–2025.
arXiv:2608.14116 — Harvard Dataverse

Matthias, L., Schares, E., Alperin, J. P., Butler, L. A., Kuang, S., Schönfelder, N., & Haustein, S. (2026). Estimating global article processing charges paid to 14 publishers for open access between 2019 and 2025. arXiv:2608.16322.

This dataset updates the six-publisher 2019–2023 dataset by Butler et al. (2024): arXiv:2406.08356 and its Harvard Dataverse dataset.
